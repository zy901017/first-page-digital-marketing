Commercially verified growth outcomes

Detailed breakdowns of strategy, execution and results across multiple sectors and spend levels.

E-Commerce / Retail

National Homewares Retailer — Google Shopping Overhaul

A high-volume retailer with $80K/month in Google Ads spend was generating a blended ROAS of 2.1× — well below their breakeven threshold of 3.5×.

Engagement Details

Industry: E-Commerce
Monthly Spend: $80K
Duration: 4 months
Channels: Google Shopping, Search

Challenge

Campaigns were structured by product category with no product-level segmentation. High-converting SKUs and low-margin SKUs were bidding identically. Attribution was broken — view-through conversions were inflating reported ROAS by 40%.

Strategic Approach

We rebuilt the campaign architecture from scratch: granular product segmentation by margin tier, revenue-based smart bidding targets set per product group, and full tracking remediation using server-side tagging to restore data accuracy.

Execution

Product feed cleansing, campaign restructure in four weeks, GA4 + server-side tracking rebuild, Looker Studio dashboard tied to Shopify revenue data. Creative assets refreshed for top-performing categories.

38%

B2B / SaaS

Enterprise Software Platform — Demand Generation Rebuild

A Series B SaaS company with $45K/month in paid search was generating high MQL volume but poor SQL conversion — disconnect between marketing signals and sales-qualified criteria.

Engagement Details

Industry: B2B SaaS
Monthly Spend: $45K
Duration: 3 months
Channels: Google Ads, LinkedIn

Challenge

CPL had risen 28% year-on-year. Campaigns were optimising toward form fills rather than pipeline-qualified leads. No offline conversion data was flowing back to Google Ads — machine learning was bidding blind.

Strategic Approach

Implemented HubSpot-to-Google Ads offline conversion sync. Rebuilt bidding strategy around SQL events rather than MQL form fills. Restructured LinkedIn campaigns by company size and vertical to align with ICP.

Execution

CRM integration build (4 weeks), campaign restructure and relaunch, creative and copy overhaul aligned with enterprise buying language, weekly SQLrate reporting dashboard delivered to marketing and sales leadership jointly.

Healthcare / Allied Health

Multi-Location Allied Health Group — Patient Acquisition

A group operating 12 clinics across NSW was running separate agency relationships per location with no unified measurement, inconsistent messaging and declining organic visibility.

Engagement Details

Industry: Allied Health
Locations: 12
Duration: 6 months
Channels: SEO, Google Ads, GBP

Challenge

No unified patient acquisition funnel. Google Business Profiles were unclaimed or incomplete. Organic rankings had declined due to technical SEO issues. Google Ads campaigns had no conversion tracking beyond phone call clicks.

Strategic Approach

Consolidated all digital into a single managed account. Rebuilt GBP profiles for all 12 locations. Implemented proper booking confirmation tracking (not just phone clicks). Technical SEO remediation and location-specific content strategy.

Execution

3-month SEO foundation build, parallel Google Ads launch with proper conversion tracking, GBP optimisation across all locations, Looker Studio patient acquisition dashboard tied to their practice management system.

3.8×
Increase in Qualified Bookings
